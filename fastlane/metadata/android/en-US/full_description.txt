PrettyDamnFine is a distraction-free, offline-first PDF reader built for focus.

Features:
- Native MuPDF rendering for crisp pages and fast scrolling.
- Real PDF highlights: select text to highlight or remove a highlight, then save.
- Library search, sorting, multi-select and deletion, plus share to any app.
- Light, dark, sepia and auto reading themes, and a home-screen light/dark toggle.
- Gesture controls: pinch zoom, double-tap zoom, read mode, fast scroll and page overview.
- Local text search with highlighted matches.
- No network access, no accounts, no analytics. Your documents stay on your device.
